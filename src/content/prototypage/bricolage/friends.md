contentEn: "Inspired by the famous door of the main apartment in the Friends series, this photo frame is made from pine boards. The two boards were cut and glued together to achieve the right thickness.\n\nThe motif was carved mainly using a router, a drill with a carving bit, and a Dremel. The sanding was done by hand.\n\nThe passe-partout was cut from pine plywood.\n\nOnce carved, the frame was spray-painted and the passe-partout was painted with a brush. Finally, the slogan \"Friends & family\" was painted onto the passe-partout with a stencil."
